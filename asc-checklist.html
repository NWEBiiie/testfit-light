How to use the references

Each card cites a subsection of §508.52 unless another section is named. Open the official rule and locate that subsection. The official portal also has a VIEW PDF control; this page links to the stable rule page rather than an expiring PDF download. For a printable copy of this checklist, clear search filters and choose Print / Save PDF.

Required means the function must be provided for the stated ASC scope, sometimes as an alcove or designated area rather than a separate room. Conditional depends on services or processing arrangements. Optional means the reviewed construction clause does not universally require that room; requirements apply when it is provided. All rooms also need applicable accessibility, fire/life-safety, finishes, plumbing, electrical and ventilation checks.

  • §508.51 existing facilities: determine whether existing, remodeled, relocated or converted facilities must meet new-construction requirements.
  • §508.54 functional program and plan review: document workload, occupancy, equipment, relationships and special requirements; obtain required review before construction.
  • 42 CFR §416.44: additional Medicare physical-environment and safety requirements.
  • Texas Accessibility Standards: accessible routes, doors, toilets, counters and other scoped features.

The current Texas text retains some legacy code editions and §135 cross-references. Confirm the applicable code matrix and interpretations with HHSC and the licensed design team.
